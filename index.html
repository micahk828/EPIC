E.P.i.C. Youth Weekend 2026

Homecoming: The Call Back

Please join us on Friday, September 25th, and Saturday, September 26th for our Youth Homecoming Services, as we welcome back our youth who may have wandered away for various reasons.

We are praying that the Holy Spirit will call them back and remind them that they are loved, valued, and always have a place to come home to. Our desire is not only to welcome them back, but to provide them with the love, support, encouragement, and resources they need to remain connected and continue growing in Christ.

We are believing God for a powerful weekend of restoration, reconnection, healing, and hope!
